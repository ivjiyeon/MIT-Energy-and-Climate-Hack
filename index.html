COMSOL Multiphysics® integration

PhaseFarm handles quick screening and economics. COMSOL handles high-fidelity physics: 2D/3D conduction through real wall assemblies, thermal bridges, convection and the PCM melt front. Use the steps below to pass data in both directions.

1

Export to COMSOL

A parameter file (Global Definitions → Parameters → Load from File) and an outdoor weather table (Definitions → Functions → Interpolation) for the selected month.

2

Compare & calibrate

Import a COMSOL indoor-temperature probe (time, T) to overlay it on the thermal chart. Calibration then fits this model's PCM film coefficient and thermal-mass multiplier to your COMSOL curve.

3

Drive costs with COMSOL loads

Import daily heating/cooling loads per strategy and month from COMSOL. The economics then use your COMSOL numbers in place of the built-in model's. Template

Which COMSOL capabilities fit this problem?
  • Heat Transfer Module: the Phase Change Material domain feature (apparent heat capacity), layered walls, thermal bridges (see the slide example), surface-to-surface radiation and solar irradiation.
  • CFD Module (non-isothermal flow): greenhouse air circulation, vent opening, airflow over PCM panels. Use it to replace the assumed film coefficient h.
  • Packed-bed latent heat storage: hot water over paraffin capsules (from the slides), applied here as a PCM tank on the nutrient-water loop.
  • Parametric / optimization studies: sweep PCM thickness, melt temperature and position, mirroring the optimizer here, then import the results (step 3).
  • LiveLink™ for MATLAB® or the MPh Python package: script whole sweeps. See run_comsol_sweep.py.
  • Application Builder + COMSOL Compiler™: wrap the wall model in a standalone app for judges and growers.
